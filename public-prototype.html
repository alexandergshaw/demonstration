Prepare an evidence-grounded PLC

A guided experience for interpreting student learning, shaping one teaching change, and checking what happened next.

Design principle: do with, never do for.

AI organizes information and tests reasoning. The educator chooses the interpretation, action, and follow-up.

No live AI or district data. All examples are fictional and de-identified.
Prototype notes: This self-contained design demonstrates the user experience with fixed example prompts, local browser state, and fictional data. It does not connect to a model, student information system, or educator records. Designed for an interview discussion, not for student-data production use.
